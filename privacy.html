Privacy Policy

Redline Training · Last updated 26 October 2026

Redline Training ("we", "us") is an online personal training and coaching business based in Queensland, Australia. This page explains what information we collect, why, and how you can have it deleted.

What we collect

  • Instagram activity. When you comment on our posts or message our Instagram account, we receive your public username, the content of your comment or message, and the time it was sent.
  • Free starter plan sign-ups. Your first name, email address, optional Instagram handle, and your training goal, experience, days per week and where you train.
  • Client app. If you become a client, we store your account email, profile, training and nutrition logs, check-ins and messages with your coach.

How we use it

  • To reply to your comments and messages, including some short automated replies to simple comments.
  • To send you your starter plan and related follow-up emails, and to coach you if you become a client.
  • To improve our content and services.

We do not sell your information. We do not use it for advertising by third parties.

Where it is stored

Information is stored with our hosting and database provider, Supabase, with access limited to the account owner and authorised coaching staff. Instagram data is received through Meta's official Instagram platform.

Your choices and data deletion

You can ask us at any time to see or delete the information we hold about you. To request deletion, email redlinepersonaltrainingau@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and include your Instagram username or the email you used. We will delete your data within 30 days and confirm by email. You can also remove our access to your Instagram data at any time from your Instagram settings under Apps and Websites.

Children

Our services are not directed at anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information.
