How we estimate this

A single photo or description is an estimate, not a lab measurement — we're upfront about that rather than pretending otherwise.

Confidence is scored honestly: composite dishes with hidden ingredients (soups, casseroles, sauces), packaged food with an unreadable label, and photos with no size reference all get marked lower confidence on purpose.

Every time you adjust a result, we remember it. The next time you log something similar, we use your corrected numbers instead of guessing again — so the meals you eat often get faster and more accurate the more you use Redline.
